# Send your first invoice

## 1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:4174/`.

![Step 1](img/1-open.png)

## 2. Click **+ New invoice**.

![Step 2](img/2-new.png)

## 3. Select **Halcyon Robotics** in **Customer**.

![Step 3](img/3-customer.png)

## 4. Type **Brand sprint, October** in the **Item** field.

![Step 4](img/4-item.png)

## 5. Type **6400** in the **Rate** field.

![Step 5](img/5-rate.png)

## 6. Click **Send invoice**.

![Step 6](img/6-send.png)
